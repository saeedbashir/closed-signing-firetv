## Found, and why it was silent

Four defects that had shipped believing they worked:

1. Preferences were never loaded or saved (`storage.ts` was wired to recents only) —
   and when wired, the Kepler fork's `AsyncStorage` turned out to be a no-op on Vega.
   friction-log/011.
2. The Opacity control never changed anything: View opacity does not reach the video
   plane, and neither does a child View over it. A root-level sibling scrim does.
   friction-log/012.
3. Resume never resumed: `resumeMs` reached the route and stopped there.
4. Play/Pause and the skip keys did nothing: the device sends `play` and `forward`;
   the SDK's type union offers `playpause` and `skip_forward`. friction-log/009.

Also the primary buttons went near-invisible when focused, `hasTVPreferredFocus` and
`TVFocusGuideView autoFocus` cannot move focus to a panel that appears over a focused
screen (friction-log/010), and Settings' rows drew ‹ › chevrons that did nothing.
